# Cook at home

## What this goal is

Replace the default of eating out with a meal you made. This is a stability goal, not “never restaurants.”

## How I want to achieve it

- Decide the meal before you are already hungry (prep, leftovers, or a simple staple).
- Log `1` if the main meal was cooked at home, `0` if you defaulted to eating out.
