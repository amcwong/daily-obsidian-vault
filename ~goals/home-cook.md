# Cook at home

## What this goal is

Replace the default of eating out with a meal you made. Health and time: you already know what is in the kitchen, and you are not waiting on delivery.

## How I want to achieve it

- Decide the main meal before you are already hungry (prep, leftovers, or a simple staple).
- Default is `1`.

## Failure condition

- Log `0` if the main meal is takeout, delivery, or a restaurant. A sandwich you assembled at home still counts as `1`.
- A planned restaurant with someone else can be `1` only if you wrote that exception in **Today** before the meal. Unplanned eating out is `0`.
